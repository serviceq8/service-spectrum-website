Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Service Spectrum ("we", "our", or "us") is a website design and management company. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide: when you contact us by email, phone, or through our contact form, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, and project description.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used only for security and performance purposes.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide quotes for our services.
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve the websites and services we manage for clients.
  • To keep our own website secure and functioning properly.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not send marketing emails without your consent.
  • Our contact form does not store your message on this website — it opens your own email application.

4. Client Data

When we design or manage a website on your behalf, we treat all business information, content, and credentials you share with us as confidential. Access is limited to what is required to perform the agreed work, and credentials are stored securely.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we ever introduce analytics tools, they will be configured to respect visitor privacy and this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Render and may load fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical data (such as IP addresses) as part of delivering their services. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

7. Data Retention

We keep correspondence and project records only as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us
